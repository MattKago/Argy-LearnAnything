# 🚀 Argsmind — Learn Anything, 15 Minutes a Day

**Argy** is an AI-native mobile learning companion that transforms any topic, syllabus, or document into structured, gamified micro-courses. Combining the habit-forming progression of Duolingo with a deep-space constellation aesthetic, Argy turns passive reading into interactive, daily retention drills.

---

### ✨ Key Features

- 🛰️ **Instant AI Curriculum Generation** — Prompt with any subject or upload study material (`.pdf`, `.pptx`, notes, images). Argy's backend pipeline analyzes the content via OCR and structured agents to generate multi-unit learning paths.
- 🎮 **Gamified Micro-Lessons** — Bite-sized daily modules with interactive mechanics:
  - 3D flip flashcards & swipe-sort drills
  - Multi-choice, fill-in-the-blank, and code-comprehension questions
  - Rendered diagrams and visual milestones
- 🤖 **Interactive AI Tutor (Voice & Chat)** — Stuck on a concept? Tap into Argy's built-in conversational tutor for real-time explanations, hints, and speech-to-text / text-to-speech audio dialogues.
- 🌌 **Constellation Path Map & Streaks** — Visual skill progression maps, daily streaks, XP counters, and energy systems that keep learners accountable.
- ⚡ **Deterministic & Offline-First Player** — Lessons are pre-generated as validated `LessonScript` schemas, ensuring instantaneous load times, zero latency mid-lesson, and offline resilience.
- 🎨 **Deep-Space Design System** — A dark-only canvas built around strict radius hierarchy, cyan glow accents, hairline surface depths, and smooth 60fps Reanimated gestures.
